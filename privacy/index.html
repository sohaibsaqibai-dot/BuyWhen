Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026 · Covers the BuyWhen browser extension and this website

In short
  • BuyWhen has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads.
  • Everything BuyWhen saves stays in your browser on your device.
  • We never sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

What BuyWhen does

BuyWhen helps you find the lowest price for a product and decide when to buy it. It compares the shopping offers shown on Google search results, keeps a price history for products you choose to follow, and alerts you when a price drops to your target.

Information the extension handles

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it is kept
Products you choose to track: page address, product name, image address, price, currency and your target priceTo check the price, show its history and alert youYour browser (chrome.storage.local) until you remove it
Price history and deal log for those productsTo show charts, the Buy or Wait verdict and past dropsYour browser, until you remove the product or delete all data
Shopping offers shown on a Google results page you are viewing (product name, price, store name and link)To rank offers from lowest to highest in the BuyWhen panelTemporary browser memory (chrome.storage.session), cleared when you close the tab or the browser
Your settings (check frequency, alert choices, default target)To remember your preferencesYour browser

BuyWhen does not collect your name, email address, payment details, passwords, location, health information or messages. It does not record your general browsing history. It only reads Google search results pages and the product pages of items you decide to track or check.

When the extension connects to websites

  • Store pages you track. To check a price, BuyWhen loads the product page directly from that store, as your browser would, including the store’s own cookies so the price matches what you normally see.
  • Product images. Images in the watchlist load from the store or image address saved with the product.
  • Track from Google. When you press Track on an offer, BuyWhen opens the store page once to read its real price. Sponsored offers open in a normal, visible tab.

These requests go straight from your browser to the website involved. BuyWhen has no server in between and receives no copy of the data.

Permissions

BuyWhen asks for access to store websites only when you choose to track or check a product there, and you can remove that access at any time in Chrome’s extension settings. It reads Google search results pages to compare the offers shown there. Notifications are used only for price alerts you turn on.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics or ads. This website does not use tracking cookies, analytics tools, advertising or third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from this website.
  • Web price tracker and price search. Products you add to the web price tracker, and the country you choose in price search, are saved only in your browser’s local storage on this device. They are never sent to us. Clear them with Delete all on the tracker page or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Links to other websites. Price search opens Google or store websites in a new tab. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use these logs.
  • Email. If you email support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Sharing and selling

BuyWhen does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to any third party. It does not use your data for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to tracking prices. No person at BuyWhen can see the data stored in your browser.

The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Remove any product from your watchlist at any time.
  • Use Settings, Delete everything in the BuyWhen dashboard to erase all products, history and the deal log.
  • Use Settings, Export to save a copy of your list for yourself.
  • Uninstalling BuyWhen removes all of its stored data from your browser.

Children

BuyWhen is a general shopping tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If BuyWhen ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated with a new effective date before the change takes effect.
